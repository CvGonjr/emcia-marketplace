    Adds two new registro/ domains, both distinct from contexto/ (CTX-01)
    and from estado.json's step machine:
    
    - registro/operacional/ (P6): OP-*.yaml specifications of where the

      decides over (operacional_ref) and only resolves against a validado
      spec, applying the P7-depends-on-P6 relationship at the field layer.

    estado.evento, estado.autor_e_agente) without extending them with
    method semantics, and both structurally block an agent from performing
    the human-only transition (validado / decidido) -- confirmed by an
    explicit hard-code search in eiac-nucleo/ after the change.
    
    New skills hb-operacionalizar (P6, EX3) and hb-governar (P7, EX4,
    delegavel:false) and their commands. Updates reference/gates.md and
    hb-emitir-e4 to drop the now-resolved E4/E5 gate pendencia (2.6.0,
    decisoes/013).
